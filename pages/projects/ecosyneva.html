EcoSyneva Commons LLC

My professional venture in environmental research and technology.

About the Venture

EcoSyneva Commons LLC is a Colorado company I founded to bring environmental research, ecological stewardship, education, and responsible technology into practical projects. It is an early-stage, founder-led venture focused on developing useful work and growing within its available skills, partnerships, and resources.

Visit EcoSyneva Commons LLC

My Role

As founder and managing member, I guide the company’s direction and develop its early projects and systems. My work draws on environmental research, GIS and remote sensing, data analysis, field experience, and software development.

Through EcoSyneva, I connect research questions with technical tools and interdisciplinary project planning. This includes building the software and ecological-computing foundations of Elysia Ecobotics, while developing the organizational capacity to support the work.

Relationship to Elysia Ecobotics

Elysia Ecobotics is a project developed and operated under EcoSyneva Commons LLC. It connects local AI, developer tools, and environmental research interests. Its dedicated portfolio case study explains the engineering work and my contribution in more detail.
